NYU Abu Dhabi — Undergraduate Capstone Research Sep 2025 – May 2026

Independent Researcher · Advisor: Dr. Sandra Siby

  • Measured how privacy behavior in Android web browsers changed over seven years by instrumenting 39 historical APKs across five browsers (2018–2025), using Frida-based SSL-pinning bypass and mitmproxy interception to recover the traffic each version sent to first- and third-party endpoints.
  • Built the analysis pipeline attributing intercepted requests to trackers and tracking per-browser drift across versions; this work forms the basis of the ongoing “Invisible Web” line of research.
