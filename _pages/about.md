I am a Computer Science researcher and recent graduate from New York University Abu Dhabi (NYUAD), where I worked at the [Center for Cyber Security (CCS)](https://sites.nyuad.nyu.edu/ccs-ad/) under the guidance of [Professor Sandra Siby](https://sandrasiby.github.io/) and [Professor Christina Pöpper](https://poepper.net/). I am currently applying to Computer Science PhD programs for Fall 2027.

**Prospective PhD Student: I am applying for Fall 2027 PhD programs in Computer Security and Privacy.**

My research sits at the intersection of web security, online privacy, and large-scale empirical measurement. I am particularly interested in how the modern web is evolving with the rise of AI, examining the security and privacy implications of LLM-driven browser agents and how we can build better measurement pipelines to inform stronger browser defenses.

Previously, my work has spanned longitudinal studies of [third-party tracking](https://petsymposium.org/popets/2026/popets-2026-0039.pdf) and client-side vulnerabilities across global government websites, historical privacy evaluations of Android mobile browsers, and anomaly detection in aviation surveillance protocols (ADS-B). I enjoy building open-source measurement tools to make empirical security research more reproducible.
